# Tech-debt register

> Every known shortcut, smell, outdated dependency or missing safety net, so it is paid down on
> purpose. Written by `tech-debt`, `tech-review` and `log-job`. Never delete a row; change the
> status and note the date. The session hook counts rows with status `open` and priority `high`,
> so keep the column order.
>
> - **Quadrant** (Martin Fowler): reckless-deliberate · prudent-deliberate · reckless-inadvertent ·
>   prudent-inadvertent
> - **Interest:** what keeping it costs (slower changes, incidents, security, onboarding) — H/M/L
> - **Effort:** to fix — S (≤1 day) / M (≤1 week) / L (more)
> - **Priority:** high / medium / low · **Status:** open / scheduled / in progress / paid / accepted
